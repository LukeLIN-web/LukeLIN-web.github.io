We release a large-scale dataset and benchmark to explore a key question: *Can video models truly reason?* 🧠🎬 (✨ **100** diverse tasks, 🎥 **1M** videos,  **Fully open-source**). Paper:**[A Very Big Video Reasoning Suite](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20159)** (co-first author)! 🎉  📦 **Dataset**: [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Video-Reason/VBVR-Dataset) 🛠 **Code**: [GitHub](https://github.com/Video-Reason/VBVR-EvalKit/tree/main).
